Waste Management & Sustainability

“A clean and healthy environment is part and parcel of the wealth and quality of life that we desire for ourselves now and for our children in the future.”

The organizers have made it their foremost priority to provide a clean, hygienic environment in and around Jawai to athletes and tourists alike. Only eco-friendly disposable plates and products will be used or offered to racers by the organizers. Post-race, it will also be ensured that total removal and proper disposal of all garbage from the base camp and the entire trail at the end of the race.

We will undertake every measure to ensure that the natural habitat remains untouched and in pristine condition for the wildlife and forest spirits to continue their peaceful existence.
