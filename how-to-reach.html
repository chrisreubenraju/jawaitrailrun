How to Reach Jawai

By Air

Jodhpur Airport (JDH) – Approximately 150 km (3 hours by road)

Maharana Pratap Airport, Udaipur (UDR) – Approximately 165 km (3.5 hours by road)

By Train

  • Jawai Bandh Railway Station – Approx. 20km
  • Falna Railway Station – Approx. 35 km
  • Abu Road Railway Station – Approx. 95 km

By Road

  • Ahmedabad – 300 km (5–6 hours)
  • Udaipur – 165 km (3–3.5 hours)
  • Jodhpur – 150 km (3 hours)
  • Jaipur – 400 km (7–8 hours)
  • Mount Abu – 110 km (2 hours)
