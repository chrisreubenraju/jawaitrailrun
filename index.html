About the Event

More Than a Run.
A New Way to Experience Jawai.

Jawai Trail Run (JTR) is envisioned as Rajasthan’s premier and first-of-its-kind trail-running experience, bringing together runners, adventure seekers, nature enthusiasts and the local community against the dramatic landscape of Jawai.

Set across granite hills, wilderness terrain, village landscapes and leopard country, JTR transforms the region into an authentic endurance and outdoor experience, where sport, nature, culture and adventure come together to create a completely new way of experiencing Jawai.

Discover Jawai
ITRA Member

Building Jawai into a Trail Running Destination.

Jawai Trail Run is proud to be associated with the global trail running community through the International Trail Running Association (ITRA).

The event is designed to offer runners an authentic trail-running experience through the unique landscapes and natural terrain of Jawai. Eligible race categories will be evaluated and listed in accordance with the applicable ITRA requirements.

Runners will have the opportunity to be part of the global trail-running community, with race results and performances managed in accordance with the relevant ITRA guidelines.
