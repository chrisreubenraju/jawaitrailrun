Discover Jawai

Where Wilderness Meets Adventure

Nestled amidst the ancient Aravalli hills of Rajasthan, Jawai is a landscape unlike any other. Known for its dramatic granite formations, rugged trails, open wilderness and rich wildlife, this remarkable destination offers an unforgettable setting for trail running.

Jawai is a place where nature sets the course. Rocky terrain, undulating paths, forest trails and wide-open landscapes create an experience that goes far beyond running on a conventional road. Every kilometre brings a new challenge, a new view and a deeper connection with the wild.

The region is internationally known for its harmonious coexistence between local communities and wildlife, particularly its thriving leopard population. The iconic granite hills, rural landscapes and serene surroundings make Jawai one of Rajasthan's most distinctive outdoor destinations.

For runners, Jawai Trail Run is an opportunity to step away from the ordinary and embrace the raw beauty of the outdoors. Whether you are taking on the longer endurance challenge or exploring the trails through a shorter distance, the journey promises adventure, discovery and unforgettable memories.
